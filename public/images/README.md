# Cookly preview imagery

These six static assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-17 for the design-system/Home milestone. They are explicitly illustrative demo imagery, not photographs of tested community recipes. No image-generation service is called by the application.

Original generated PNGs remain outside the repository. The project uses optimized WebP copies (food cards: 800px wide; hero: 1400px; kitchen: original width up to 1800px; quality 82). The approved design references were not modified.

## Prompts

Every prompt starts with: “Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: Cookly static demo website asset.” and ends with: “No text, no typography, no UI, no logos, no people, no watermark.”

- **lemon-pasta.webp**: Wide editorial food photograph, creamy lemon herb tagliatelle piled in a rustic ivory ceramic bowl, delicate parmesan shavings, parsley and lemon zest. Three-quarter close-up, bowl filling RIGHT two thirds, left third softly blurred light kitchen and herbs for text overlay. Sunlit cream and olive palette, appetizing natural texture. Landscape 3:2.

- **salmon.webp**: Editorial food photograph of miso glazed salmon fillet over sauteed greens in a dark ceramic bowl, sesame and scallion garnish. Tight three-quarter view, dish centered, sunlit warm kitchen bokeh. Landscape 3:2.

- **pancakes.webp**: Editorial food photograph of a small stack of fluffy banana pancakes with raspberries, blackberries and a drizzle of maple syrup on cream ceramic plate. Tight three-quarter view, dish centered, sunlit warm kitchen bokeh. Landscape 3:2.

- **grain-bowl.webp**: Editorial food photograph of Mediterranean grain bowl: quinoa, sliced avocado, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, chickpeas and herbs, in olive-green ceramic bowl. Tight three-quarter view, dish centered, soft sunlit kitchen. Landscape 3:2.

- **tomato-soup.webp**: Editorial food photograph of roasted tomato soup in rustic tan ceramic bowl topped with basil and a small swirl of cream, crusty bread at edge. Tight three-quarter view, dish centered, warm sunlit kitchen. Landscape 3:2.

- **kitchen.webp**: Airy high-key blurred kitchen background for a recipe website, cream plaster walls, sunlight through tall windows on left, soft green herb foliage along extreme left and right edges, pale oak counter along bottom edge, a bowl of lemons in bottom left corner. CENTER 75 percent is very quiet soft ivory negative space behind readable HTML content. Wide landscape 3:2. Realistic editorial interior photography, intentional depth-of-field blur.
